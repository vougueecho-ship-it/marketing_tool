📩 How to Add Gmail Account:

  1. Enable 2-Step Verification in Google Account Security.
  2. Go to Google Account -> Search "App Passwords".
  3. Generate a 16-character password (e.g., abcd efgh ijkl mnop).
  4. Click + Add Email Account above, select Gmail Preset, enter your Gmail & 16-char App Password!

🛡️ Anti-Spam Best Practices Built-in:

  • 80 Daily Cap per Account: Prevents accounts from getting flagged by Gmail/Hostinger filters.
